## About me
I'm a methodical problem solver who wants to understand how systems work, how they fail, and what happened when they did. My strengths are structuring complex systems and data, working through problems step by step until I find the cause, and explaining technical issues in plain language. I value getting to the truth and being reliable, which is why I'm drawn to the investigative side of cybersecurity: examining the evidence, reconstructing events, and documenting findings that others can trust and act on. My curiosity about how attackers find weaknesses in code helps me understand what went wrong and how to prevent it. I want to apply this as a security analyst or incident responder at an organisation that handles sensitive data and values careful, well-documented analysis.
